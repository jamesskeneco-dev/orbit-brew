How Orbit Brew Works

What the subscription is, how it works, and why we built it.

The idea

Orbit Brew started with a simple problem: good cold brew is expensive at a café and slow to make at home. A single café cold brew can cost five or six dollars, and making it yourself takes a full day of steeping and a lot of cleanup. Orbit Brew solves both problems with a subscription that delivers fresh cold brew concentrate straight to your door.

We steep specialty-grade beans in cold, filtered water for 18 hours, then filter and bottle the concentrate the same day. Each 32 oz glass bottle makes about eight drinks, which works out to roughly $1.50 a cup. That is much less than a café cold brew.

Getting started

  1. Find your blend. Take the Blend Finder quiz on our home page or ask Orbi, our barista chatbot, for a recommendation.
  2. Choose your orbit. Pick a delivery every two or four weeks. Pause, skip or cancel whenever you like.
  3. Mix and enjoy. Combine one part concentrate with two parts water, milk or tonic. Unopened bottles stay fresh for three weeks in the fridge.
  4. Close the loop. Mail your empty bottles back in the prepaid sleeve and get a $2 credit for each one. We sanitize and reuse every bottle.

Our mission

Most bottled coffee ends up as single-use plastic. Orbit Brew uses only returnable glass bottles, and our goal is for every bottle to make at least ten trips before it is recycled. We buy our beans from importers who publish what they pay farmers, and we roast in small batches so that every delivery tastes fresh.

Our target customers are busy students and young professionals who want café-quality coffee at home without the cost or the waste. Over time we plan to add seasonal limited blends and office plans for teams.
